## Next, in order

3. **Fused latent head** (done: run14 and the run15 control both flat). Both variants keep run11's linear readout and add a residual MLP (1024 -> 2048 -> 128, last layer zero-initialised, so the warm start from run11 reproduces 31.27 dB at step 0; verified bit-exact on real slices). `LATENT_HEAD=mlp`: the MLP reads the body's normalised hidden state only (readout-capacity test). `LATENT_HEAD=fused`: the MLP reads [body hidden state (1024) | token head's chain state (1024, the mean of its normalised hidden state over the 8 chain steps) | tokenizer codebook latents of the token head's codes (2 x 64)]. In training the token head is rolled out greedily (no gradient, no KV cache; 0.44 s per batch of 8, about 15 percent of a step); at inference the fused head reads the codes the token path actually committed to (greedy or sampled). Launch: `LATENT=1 LATENT_HEAD=fused MODEL_VERSION=2.1 HEAD_IL=0 TOKEN_SIZE=512 EPOCHS=3 LR=5e-5 BS=8 WARM_START=<run11 best_final.pt> RUN=run14_v21_fused bash train_sense.sh` (then `LATENT_HEAD=mlp` as the control). Files: `gpt_kar.py` (`latent_head_type`, `rollout_head`, `set_latent_codebook`, `infer_model_args_from_state_dict`), `train.py` (`--latent-head-type`), `generate.py` (head states collected in the sampler loop), `tests/test_latent_head.py`.
4. **Full 320 grid** (running as run16). v2.1 with the latent head trained at the native 320 grid (13 ACS lines, ~40 sampled lines), the setting where ~34 dB vs SENSE is realistic (run4 at 320 with v1: 32.50 dB PS8; leaderboard VarNet there: 34.25 dB). Use the 320-tuned sensitivity net (`results/sens_net/run320/best.pt`), CROP_SIZE=320 TOKEN_SIZE=512.
5. **Progressive-commitment refinement.** Pass 1, keep the confident positions, re-predict the rest with the kept positions as the second view, over 2-3 rounds. Confidence = expected-embedding spread (1 - |expected chunk embedding|^2, summed over the 16 chunks), to be validated against posterior-sampling draw disagreement. Needs partial-draft training (random per-position drop of the draft view). How to commit (schedule, fraction, score) to be discussed before implementation.
   Confidence diagnostic (Oct 28, no training; `scripts/diag_commit_confidence.py`, `results/diag_commit_confidence/run11_fg/summary.txt`): on 80 slices of run11, inside the head (71 percent of the positions) the expected-embedding concentration (1 minus the latent spread) has rank correlation -0.88 with the latent distance of the greedy codes to the ground truth and -0.84 with the decoded patch error; top-1 probability, entropy and agreement across 8 draws have none (0.0 to -0.3). Keeping the 40 percent most confident foreground positions keeps positions with 30 percent of the average patch error (oracle 21 percent), 84 percent of them the oracle's picks. Confident positions are NOT more often the exact ground-truth code (chunk accuracy 6 percent everywhere): the product quantiser has near-duplicate codes, so top-1 probability splits across them while the expected embedding does not; the commit criterion is the latent spread.
6. **Privileged-teacher distillation** (done: run17, +0.05 dB, null at the default weights). The EMA teacher sees the same scan at x2-x4 (student's lines plus extra random lines); the x8 student is trained toward the teacher's soft token distribution (KL) and latent (L2) next to the ground-truth loss. Needs mixed-acceleration batches (x4 samples get the ground-truth loss only).
