# SENSE GPT run log

One row per training run of the SENSE pipeline (sensitivity net -> SENSE combine -> MedITok tokens -> GPT-L -> decode -> per-coil DC), in launch order. "Adds" is the one change against the parent run. Numbers are PSNR vs the SENSE ground truth on the suno test split (15 volumes, 240 slices), x8, 256 grid unless the row says 320. "Validation" is the training-time validation of the best epoch (latent one pass where the model has a latent head, otherwise greedy + DC; all validations use the old resize DC path, so compare validations with validations). "Full evaluation" is `eval_sense_block.py` on the frozen best checkpoint with the plain acquisition-grid DC (the table standard since Oct 26) unless marked "old DC". Evaluation directories are under `results/sense_block/`. The detailed tables are in `results/sense_summary_256.md`, `results/sense_summary_320.md` and `results/advisor_summary_2026-10-26.md`; the queue is in `results/sense_run_queue.md`.

Vocabulary: **body** = GPT-L (426M) transformer; **token head** = the 8-step autoregressive head over the 8 codebooks (v1: shared table, summed feedback; v2.1: stream-aware, separate tables for the Re and Im codes, concatenated and projected); **latent head** = RMSNorm -> Linear(1024 -> 128) regressing the tokenizer's pre-quantisation latent (2 streams x 8 unit-norm chunks), decoded without quantisation; **PS8** = posterior mean of 8 sampled token reconstructions; **hybrid** = latent head below a normalised k-space radius, one sampled token reconstruction above, merged in k-space before DC.

## Decoding-side findings (no training; apply to every model above)

- **Plain acquisition-grid DC** (Oct 26): the "+ DC" arms used to apply the per-coil DC inside a resize round trip (512 -> 256, DC, bicubic up, antialiased down), and the resize after DC is a low-pass. Plain DC on the 256 grid: +0.08 dB and 0.025 better LPIPS on run11's latent path (0.156 -> 0.131); at 320 it costs 0.4 dB PSNR but improves LPIPS. Table standard since Oct 26 (`--dc-path acq`).
- **Hybrid decoding** (Oct 26): latent head below k-space radius 0.5, one sampled token reconstruction above. run11: 30.98 dB at LPIPS / DISTS 0.115 / 0.131 against VarNet's 32.06 at 0.124 / 0.147; run18 at 320: 32.67 at 0.084 / 0.117.
- **Latent one pass vs PS8** at 256: the one pass wins on 15 of 15 volumes (mean +0.15 dB) for run11; at 320 the PS8 wins (33.29 vs 32.99 for run18).
- **Mask transfer** (run11, Oct 26): equispaced-fraction mask at the same budget 31.83 one pass (VarNet 32.61); standard fastMRI equispaced mask (41 columns) 32.74 (VarNet 33.46).
- **Commit-confidence diagnostic** (Oct 28, for the progressive-commitment plan): on 80 slices of run11, inside the head (71 percent of the positions) the expected-embedding concentration (1 minus the latent spread) has rank correlation -0.88 with the latent distance of the greedy codes to the ground truth and -0.84 with the decoded patch error; top-1 probability, entropy and agreement across 8 draws have none (0.0 to -0.3). Keeping the 40 percent most confident foreground positions keeps positions with 30 percent of the average patch error (oracle 21 percent), 84 percent of them the oracle's picks. Confident positions are NOT more often the exact ground-truth code (chunk accuracy 6 percent everywhere): the product quantiser has near-duplicate codes, so top-1 probability splits across them while the expected embedding does not; the commit criterion is the latent spread.
